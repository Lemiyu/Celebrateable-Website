About Us

We're not an events company. We're a community.

A place where disabled and neurodivergent families find belonging, friendship and somewhere they never have to explain themselves.

Our mission

Communities where disabled and neurodivergent families feel accepted, not forgotten.

Our vision

A world where no family has to stay home. Where inclusion is simply normal.

Our purpose

To remove loneliness and build friendships that last beyond the day.

It didn't make him bitter.
It made him determined.

Ben Kelly founded CelebrateAble in 2026 from lived experience. As a disabled person, he spent years on the outside looking in.

So he started building the spaces he wished had existed. Places where nobody has to explain themselves, and where belonging isn't a special occasion. It's simply normal.
