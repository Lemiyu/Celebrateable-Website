No family wants their child to be on the outside looking in.

We know what it feels like to be left out. Most families aren't looking for an event. They're looking for somewhere they fit in. Somewhere they don't have to explain their child.

CelebrateAble exists to change that by creating a community where everybody belongs and feels included.

Ben Kelly founded CelebrateAble in 2026 from lived experience. As a disabled person, he spent years on the outside looking in.

So he started building the spaces he wished had existed. Places where nobody has to explain themselves, and where belonging isn't a special occasion. It's simply normal.

Our events are designed with disabled and neurodivergent people at the centre from the very beginning, not adapted at the last minute. Sensory considerations, accessible venues, understanding staff, and a community of people who simply get it.

Our community events are open to disabled and neurodivergent children, young people, adults and their families. You don't need a formal diagnosis to attend.
